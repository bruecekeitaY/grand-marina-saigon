Grand Marina Towers Compared: Lake, Lagoon, Cove & Sea

Grand Marina Saigon has four towers across two Marriott brands and two collections. This guide compares each tower by brand, design firm and unit mix so you can choose the building that fits you.

  1. Home
  2. News

When researching Grand Marina Saigon, many buyers discover the project is not a single building but four towers: Lake, Lagoon, Cove and Sea — split across two Marriott brands (Marriott and JW Marriott) and two collections (Prime and Legacy). "Which tower should I buy?" therefore matters just as much as which unit type. This article compares each tower by operating brand, design firm, handover phase and unit character, helping you narrow down the right building before you look at a live availability list.

How many towers does Grand Marina have, and how do they differ?

Grand Marina Saigon has four towers — Lake carries the Marriott brand (Prime Collection), while Lagoon, Cove and Sea carry the JW Marriott brand (Legacy Collection).

The biggest difference is the operating brand. Marriott and JW Marriott both belong to Marriott International, but JW Marriott is the higher-tier line within the group. At Grand Marina this tiering shows up as two "collections": the Prime Collection (Lake tower, Marriott) and the Legacy Collection (Lagoon, Cove, Sea, JW Marriott). All four towers have been handed over and are now occupied, the Marriott concierge team is operating, and the resale (secondary) market has been fairly active through 2024–2026.

What makes the Lake tower (Marriott — Prime Collection) special?

Lake is the only Marriott-branded tower, part of Phase 2, standing 47 floors / 176.4 m, with interiors by HBA, and it was handed over first in March 2023.

Lake sits within the Prime Collection and stands out as the project's first tower to be handed over (March 2023), meaning its unit pool has been "live" longer than the others. The interior designer is HBA — a major name in luxury hospitality design worldwide. The contractor was Newtecons and the sub-developer was Hưng Phát Investment, on a 15,723.8 m² land area.

  • Brand: Marriott (Prime Collection)
  • Scale: 47 floors, 176.4 m tall
  • Interior design: HBA
  • Handover: March 2023
  • Unit mix: diverse — 1BR (72 units), 2BR (60), 3BR (65), Dual-Key (44), Penthouse (2), plus Officetel and Shop

Lake's unit mix is fairly balanced across sizes, suiting both owner-occupiers and investors who want options. Its share of larger homes (3BR, Dual-Key, Penthouse) is notably higher than some Legacy towers that lean toward 1BR units.

If you want a clearer sense of the layouts and sizes across the towers, browse our Residences & layouts page to picture each unit line.

How do Lagoon, Cove and Sea (JW Marriott — Legacy Collection) differ?

Lagoon, Cove and Sea all carry the JW Marriott brand within the Legacy Collection, all belong to Phase 1, and were all handed over in December 2023.

This is the higher-positioned brand group, sharing JW Marriott operation and a simultaneous late-2023 handover. While they share a collection, each tower has its own character:

  • Cove: 47 floors / 184.2 m — architecture by ATKINS (UK), interiors by AB Concept (Hong Kong). Its mix leans heavily to smaller homes: 1BR "Legacy Superior Suite" makes up about 49%, 2BR "Legacy Executive Suite" about 26%, plus Dual-Key and many Officetel units.
  • Lagoon: combines apartments with Officetel, suiting buyers who want flexibility between living and shorter-term leasing.
  • Sea: JW Marriott apartments plus a Marriott-branded Officetel line — an interesting option if you want an officetel product within the complex.

Because Cove has such a large share of 1BR units, it tends to draw rental investors; Lagoon and Sea appeal to those seeking flexible officetel space. Which tower should you choose for your goals?

Owner-occupiers who prefer larger homes often favor Lake; rental investors targeting smaller units often look at Cove; and those needing flexible officetel space usually consider Lagoon and Sea.

Tower choice should follow your real goals rather than brand alone. A few common pointers:

  • Owner-occupier / family: Lake (Marriott) has more large 3BR and Dual-Key homes and was handed over first — a settled community.
  • Rental investment: Cove (JW Marriott), with its high share of 1BR units, often offers easier rental liquidity.
  • Flexible live-and-work: Lagoon and Sea with their Officetel lines.
  • Highest brand positioning: the three Legacy towers carrying JW Marriott.

Keep in mind that rental performance and liquidity depend on the unit type, timing, floor position and broader policy — they are not the same across every tower. To match "which tower" with "which unit," you may also read Which Grand Marina Unit Rents Best? Investor's Layout Guide.

How much do prices differ between the towers?

There is no fixed price gap between towers; pricing depends on unit type, floor, orientation and sales phase, and all figures are indicative and need to be reconfirmed.

Per the indicative data on this site, price ranges by unit type (not by tower) currently sit around: 1BR from about VND 20 billion, 2BR from about VND 35 billion, 3BR from about VND 60 billion, and Sky Villa from about VND 100 billion. Because JW Marriott (Legacy) is positioned above Marriott (Prime), the Legacy towers may in theory carry slightly higher pricing, but the real gap still depends on the available stock at any given time on the secondary market. According to research firms such as Knight Frank and Savills (around 2023–2024), branded residences are typically priced 25–35% above comparable non-branded products — this is a market reference, not a promise of appreciation. Actual outcomes depend on the project, timing and policy.

Frequently asked questions about choosing a tower

Most buyers wonder about three things: how Marriott differs from JW Marriott, whether an early-handover tower is preferable, and which tower still has the most availability.

On brand, JW Marriott is the higher-tier line within the same Marriott International group; at Grand Marina this shows as the Legacy Collection (Lagoon, Cove, Sea) versus the Prime Collection (Lake). On timing, all four towers are already handed over, so any "earlier/later" difference now mainly comes down to how settled the community is and the depth of the resale pool. On availability, because the resale market is active, unit counts and prices shift constantly and should be checked directly.

Every comparison in this article is general; your final decision should rest on the availability list, legal documents and the specific price list for each tower at the time you buy.
