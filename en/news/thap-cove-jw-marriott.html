Cove Tower at Grand Marina: the tallest JW Marriott tower at 184.2m

A deep dive into Cove — the tallest tower at Grand Marina Saigon, with ATKINS (UK) architecture, AB Concept (Hong Kong) interiors and JW Marriott-branded Legacy Suite units.

  1. Home

  3. Cove Tower JW Marriott

Among the four towers at Grand Marina Saigon, each has its own character — and Cove is the one buyers ask about most when they care about the landmark statement. This article focuses on Cove alone: its height, its design firms, its Legacy Suite unit mix, and why many buyers are choosing it on the 2024–2026 resale market.

What is the Cove Tower within Grand Marina?

Cove is one of three Phase 1 towers at Grand Marina Saigon, carries the JW Marriott brand, and is the project's tallest tower at 47 floors and 184.2m.

Grand Marina Saigon is Vietnam's first Marriott & JW Marriott Branded Residences development, built by Masterise Homes Profile: Developer of Grand Marina at Ba Son, No. 02 Ton Duc Thang, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, HCMC. The project comprises four towers: Lake, Lagoon, Cove and Sea.

Cove sits in Phase 1 alongside Lagoon and Sea (Phase 1 land area is 16,866.5 m²). The entire Cove tower was handed over in December 2023, residents have moved in, and the Marriott concierge team is operating. If you want to compare Cove with the other two Legacy towers of Phase 1, read Lagoon & Sea Towers: JW Marriott Plus Officetel.

Why Cove is the tallest tower — 184.2m, 47 floors

At 184.2m across 47 floors, Cove rises above even the Lake tower (176.4m) and becomes the height landmark of the entire Grand Marina.

That height matters in practice for buyers: the higher you go, the more open and unobstructed the views over the Saigon River and the Thu Thiem peninsula become. The upper floors of Cove are generally regarded as the "best-view" positions in the project, especially for buyers seeking a long-term home or a long-term rental hold.

ATKINS & AB Concept: the design duo behind Cove

Cove's exterior massing was shaped by the architecture firm ATKINS (UK), while the Hong Kong studio AB Concept delivered the interior design to JW Marriott standards.

ATKINS is an international name in high-rise design, giving Cove a slender architectural form oriented towards the river. AB Concept — a studio renowned for premium hotel and restaurant projects across Asia — was responsible for creating common spaces that feel exactly like "living inside a JW Marriott hotel." This is the core differentiator of the branded residences model.

Legacy Suite units & the product mix at Cove

Cove is weighted heavily towards the one-bedroom "Legacy Superior Suite" (about 49% of the inventory), alongside two-bedroom, Dual-Key and Officetel units.

  • 1BR — Legacy Superior Suite: 211 units (~49%)
  • 2BR — Legacy Executive Suite: 113 units (~26%)
  • Dual-Key — Legacy Family Suite: 18 units (~4%)
  • Officetel Studio: 66 units, plus 1BR / 2BR / Dual-Key Officetel types
  • Shophouse: 7 podium units

The high share of 1BR units makes Cove well suited to rental investors and to singles or couples buying to live, while the Dual-Key units are flexible for multi-generational families or a live-and-lease combination. Indicative areas: 1BR around 50–60 m² and 2BR around 70–90 m² (figures are indicative and change by sales phase).

Every unit at Cove is handed over fully fitted to Marriott standard: stone/engineered-wood floors, Poggenpohl/Boffi kitchens with Miele/Gaggenau appliances, Toto/Duravit/Hansgrohe bathrooms, Daikin VRV air conditioning and an app-based smart-home system. You can read a full project overview on the About the project page.

How Cove differs from "Marriott" — and why the JW matters

Cove carries the JW Marriott brand (the more upscale Legacy Collection), unlike the Lake tower, which carries the Marriott brand (Prime Collection).

Within the Marriott ecosystem, JW Marriott is positioned a tier above the standard Marriott brand, and that is reflected in how each tower at Grand Marina is positioned. To understand the difference and how it affects the living experience, read Marriott vs JW Marriott: The Difference at Grand Marina.

Whichever brand they carry, Cove residents all enjoy Marriott-operated services: 24/7 concierge, in-residence dining from the JW Marriott kitchen, housekeeping & laundry, valet parking, multi-layer 24/7 security, plus Marriott Bonvoy benefits across 8,000+ hotels. The Marriott operating contract runs for 20 years (the developer subsidises the management fee for the first 3 years).

Indicative pricing, ownership & potential at Cove

Indicative prices at Grand Marina start from about VND 20 billion for a 1BR and about VND 35 billion for a 2BR, with foreigners eligible for 50-year ownership (renewable by law) and a 30% cap of units per building.

Vietnamese buyers receive a long-term pink book with full rights to use, transfer, lease and inherit, and can borrow up to 70% from banks. Foreigners are granted 50-year ownership with bilingual paperwork support. Some fees to note: 10% VAT, a one-off 2% maintenance fee, and a management fee of roughly USD 8–9/m²/month (waived for the first 3 years).

On value potential, market references from Knight Frank / Savills (in recent years) indicate that branded residences are typically priced about 25–35% higher than comparable non-branded products. This is a market reference, not a promise; actual outcomes depend on the project, timing and policy. We do not guarantee any return or claim that prices will definitely appreciate.

Each sales phase has a different inventory and price level — especially for the Cove tower, which is now trading on the secondary market.
