The Sky Infinity Pool Facing the Saigon River at Grand Marina

High up in Grand Marina Saigon, the Sky Infinity Pool looks straight out over the Saigon River, its water seeming to spill toward the river and the Thu Thiem peninsula beyond — a space reserved for residents of these Marriott branded residences.

For many buyers weighing a high-end home in central Ho Chi Minh City, a pool is not just a box to tick — it is part of daily life. At Grand Marina Saigon, Vietnam's first Marriott & JW Marriott Branded Residences at Ba Son in District 1, the amenity people talk about most is the rooftop infinity pool facing the Saigon River. This article focuses on that experience alone: the river-facing design, the jacuzzi, the kids pool and the poolside bar, and why a residents-only sky pool feels nothing like a generic condo pool.

Where is the Grand Marina infinity pool and what does it face?

Grand Marina's Sky Infinity Pool sits high on the tower as an infinity-edge pool facing straight out toward the Saigon River and the Thu Thiem peninsula across the water.

The project stands at No. 02 Ton Duc Thang, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1 — only about 200 m from the Saigon River. That riverside position lets the pool be set on the river-facing side, so its vanishing edge appears to merge with the river below. Directly opposite lies the 657-hectare Thu Thiem New Urban Area, which keeps the view from the pool wide and largely unobstructed by neighbouring buildings. That is rare for an inner-city District 1 project, where most pools look out onto a lightwell or the high-rise next door.

What does the Sky Infinity Pool actually include?

The pool deck is more than a single infinity pool — it pairs the main pool with a kids/family pool, a bubbling jacuzzi and a poolside bar serving right at the water.

  • Main infinity pool — the river-facing water for swimming and unwinding, aimed at adults.
  • Kids / family pool — a shallower depth where children can play safely, set apart from the main pool.
  • Jacuzzi — a warm bubbling spa to ease the body after a long day.
  • Poolside bar — a drinks counter right beside the water, so residents can enjoy a refreshment without leaving the deck.

This layout turns the pool deck into a relaxation hub for the whole family in one space: parents can swim or soak in the jacuzzi while keeping an eye on the children in the family pool, then order drinks together at the poolside bar. It is one part of the project's broad set of Amenities & services for residents.

How does a residents-only sky pool differ from a typical condo pool?

The biggest difference is that the Sky Infinity Pool serves residents only and is run by the Marriott team, so it stays low-density, clean and managed to five-star hotel standards.

In many mass-market condos the pool sits on a low floor, looks out onto a courtyard or internal road, gets crowded at weekends and is run by a standard building-management team. At Grand Marina the pool is lifted high with a river view, kept exclusive to residents and operated to Marriott standards — alongside services such as In-Residence Dining: JW Marriott Room Service at Home 24/7. Why does a rooftop pool add to the "branded residence" value?

A river-facing sky pool is part of the branded-residence positioning — shared spaces designed and run to hotel standards, which contributes to the premium that branded residences command.

When people hear "branded residence" they often think only of the name on the door. But most of the value comes from the daily experience: from understanding what are branded residences? to how amenities like the pool, sky lounge and spa are actually operated. Research houses such as Knight Frank and Savills have noted that branded residences worldwide are typically priced around 25–35% above comparable non-branded homes (a market reference from recent years, not a promise for any individual unit). Treat this as context for the product's positioning, not a guarantee of returns — actual outcomes depend on the project, timing and policy.

How does the poolside experience connect to other amenities?

The pool deck sits within a rooftop amenity ecosystem, linking to the spa & wellness suite, sky lounge, Technogym fitness studio and Marriott concierge services.

An afternoon by the pool at Grand Marina rarely ends with a swim. Afterwards residents can move on to the spa & wellness area — massage, sauna, steam and herbal bath — or work out in the studio described in Spa, Sauna & Technogym Fitness: Wellness Living at Home. All of it is backed by the Marriott team — from small touches like fresh towels and drinks at the poolside bar to the broader support you can read about in What a 24/7 Marriott Concierge Actually Does for You. It is this seamless flow between pool, wellness and service that creates a resort-style life in the heart of District 1.
