Spa, Sauna & Technogym Fitness: Wellness Living at Home

A complete wellness tour of Grand Marina Saigon — from spa massage, sauna and herbal bath to the Technogym fitness center and yoga, pilates and boxing studios — where looking after your health becomes a daily habit, right inside the building you live in.

In branded residences, wellness is no longer just "a gym in the lobby" — it is a body-and-mind ecosystem run to hotel standards. At Grand Marina Saigon — Vietnam's first Marriott & JW Marriott Branded Residences, set on the riverside at Ba Son, District 1 — the spa, sauna, steam and fitness facilities are designed like a private resort in miniature. This article walks you through that wellness journey, kept distinct from the pool deck, so you can picture what a "recharge day" at home really looks like.

What does the wellness cluster at Grand Marina include?

Grand Marina Saigon offers a complete wellness cluster: a spa (massage, sauna, steam, herbal bath), a fitness center equipped with Technogym, and dedicated studios for yoga, pilates, functional training and boxing.

Rather than a single multipurpose gym, the wellness offering is split into separate zones so residents can move from intense exercise to deep relaxation within the same session:

  • Spa & wellness: massage rooms, a dry sauna, a wet steam room, and a herbal bath area.
  • Technogym fitness center: a cardio and strength zone fitted with equipment from the Italian brand Technogym.
  • Multipurpose studios: space for yoga, pilates, functional classes and boxing.

Separating the spa, gym and studios into distinct spaces means residents never have to "share a slot" between weightlifters, yoga practitioners and those who simply want to unwind — a clear difference from a standard commercial condo, where every activity is usually squeezed into one room.

Spa, sauna and steam: resort-grade relaxation at home

The Grand Marina spa brings massage, dry sauna, wet steam and herbal bathing right into the building, so residents can unwind without booking an outside spa.

After a working day in central District 1, instead of travelling to a commercial spa, residents can simply head down to the wellness floor. A familiar relaxation sequence is: warm up in the dry sauna, move to the wet steam room to loosen muscles and cleanse the skin, then finish with a herbal bath or a relaxing massage.

A major plus is privacy and security. The entire amenity zone operates inside Marriott's 24/7 multi-layer security system (CCTV, card and biometric access control), so the relaxation space is reserved for residents and their guests — not a crowded public facility. This is part of why the what are branded residences? model is positioned above ordinary apartments: services and amenities are delivered to 5-star hotel standards.

Technogym fitness: an internationally equipped gym

The fitness center at Grand Marina is equipped with Technogym — a brand trusted by many 5-star hotels and premium gyms around the world.

Technogym is an Italian fitness-equipment brand known for its sleek cardio and strength machines and app connectivity for tracking workouts. With a gym right inside the building, keeping up an exercise routine becomes far easier — no more "it's raining, the traffic is bad" excuses to skip a session, because you only need to take the lift downstairs.

The gym caters to all three resident profiles: those who just want light morning cardio, serious weight trainers, and older residents who want to stay active safely. The fitness space also sits beside the river-facing Sky Infinity Pool, so some residents combine a morning gym session with a relaxing swim — you can read more about the pool and play areas in our article on Family & Kids Facilities: Kids Club, Pools & Playground.

Yoga, pilates and boxing studios: training for every style

Beyond the gym, Grand Marina provides dedicated studios for yoga, pilates, functional training and boxing, serving a wide range of fitness styles.

Not everyone enjoys lifting weights. Some residents prioritise the flexibility and balance of yoga, others choose pilates to strengthen the core, while an active younger crowd prefers the intensity of boxing and functional training. Having dedicated studios means these groups never compete for space and can run group classes whenever the resident community wants them.

Altogether, the fitness–studio–spa cluster forms a seamless wellness loop: warm up and train in the gym or studio, then recover in the sauna and spa. This is exactly the "living as if on holiday" experience that branded residences are built around.

Wellness and the management fee: how the cost works

Operating and maintaining a hotel-grade wellness cluster is part of the service package covered by the management fee of roughly 8–9 USD/m²/month, which the developer subsidises for the first three years.

A Technogym gym and a spa run to 5-star standards require a matching team and maintenance budget. At Grand Marina, this is part of the Marriott-managed service package — alongside 24/7 concierge, security and upkeep of common areas. Why is in-house wellness a plus when buying or renting?

A hotel-grade wellness cluster lifts daily quality of life for residents while also appealing to premium tenants, making the unit easier to lease.

According to market research firms such as Knight Frank and Savills (data published around 2023–2024), branded residences are typically priced 25–35% higher than comparable non-branded apartments — partly thanks to exactly these hotel-standard services and amenities, such as this wellness cluster. This is a market reference, not a promise; actual results depend on the project, timing and policy.

For owner-occupiers, the biggest value is convenience: self-care becomes a habit rather than something you have to schedule. For investors, a complete amenity set is one reason premium tenants (expatriate professionals, affluent families) choose a building — contributing to the indicative rents and yields cited in the project materials. Actual rent levels and yields vary with the market and should be confirmed at the time of purchase.
