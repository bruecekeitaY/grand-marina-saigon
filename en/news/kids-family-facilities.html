Family & Kids Facilities at Grand Marina Saigon

A supervised Kids Club, a Mommy & Baby lounge, a family pool and an outdoor playground — all designed so families with children can live safely and comfortably in the heart of District 1.

  1. Home
  2. News

When choosing a branded residence for the whole family, parents tend to care about more than bedroom size or the view. The real questions are: will my child have a safe place to play, will mom and baby have a private space, and can the family enjoy the amenities without ever leaving the building? Grand Marina Saigon — Vietnam's first Marriott & JW Marriott Branded Residences, located at Ba Son in District 1, HCMC — is planned with a dedicated cluster of facilities for children and families, kept distinct from the adult wellness and leisure spaces. This article walks through each one so your family can picture daily life here.

What family- and kid-specific facilities does Grand Marina offer?

Grand Marina Saigon provides an indoor Kids Club, an outdoor playground, a dedicated kids/family pool and a separate Mommy & Baby lounge — forming a complete amenity cluster for households with young children.

Unlike many projects that offer only a small, token play corner, Grand Marina takes a "branded residences" approach — meaning the amenity standard is designed and operated to Marriott hotel standards. The family facilities sit across the podium and landscaped areas, near the Central Lake and the Riverside Garden, so parents can easily keep an eye on children as they play.

How does the Kids Club work?

The Kids Club at Grand Marina is a supervised indoor play space that lets children stay active and socialise safely within the residence.

The Kids Club is set within the shared amenity block, a place where children can take part in play and creative activities in an environment controlled for security and hygiene. Because the entire project runs under Marriott management with 24/7 concierge and security, parents can feel more at ease than with public playgrounds outside. It is also a real plus for families who want their children to have friends of a similar age right inside the resident community.

The amenities commonly found in the children-and-family cluster include:

  • Indoor Kids Club — a covered play area, sheltered from sun and rain year-round.
  • Outdoor playground — set within the landscaped grounds, near the central lake and riverside garden.
  • Kids/family pool — separate from the adults' Sky Infinity Pool.
  • Mommy & Baby lounge — a private space for mother and child.

The clear separation between children's areas and adult spaces (such as the Sky Lounge, the Cigar Lounge & Whisky Bar, or the Technogym fitness floor) is exactly what makes Grand Marina suited to multi-generational families: grandparents relax, parents work out, and the children have a play area of their own.

How does the family pool differ from the adults' infinity pool?

Grand Marina has a river-facing Sky Infinity Pool for adults and a separate kids/family pool with a shallower, safer depth for children.

Splitting the pools into two zones addresses a common parental worry: small children swimming alongside adults in deep water. The family pool is designed with a moderate depth, while parents can still enjoy the infinity pool overlooking the Saigon River, the jacuzzi and the poolside bar. Both sit within the project grounds, so families never need to go elsewhere to find a safe place for their child to swim.

If you'd like to know exactly where the family pool sits and how large it is within each of the Lake, Lagoon, Cove or Sea towers, message us for the detailed amenity floor plan.

How important are the Mommy & Baby lounge and family privacy?

The Mommy & Baby lounge is a private space for mother and child that, combined with multi-layer security, lets families with young children feel both comfortable and safe.

For families with a baby or toddler, a quiet, discreet space to care for the child is very valuable yet rare in residential projects. The Mommy & Baby lounge at Grand Marina is built for exactly that need. Beyond that, the whole family's privacy is protected by card- and biometric-based access control, CCTV and an on-duty security team — you can read more in Security & Privacy: Multi-Layer Protection for Residents. This matters especially for families who are public figures or business owners and want their children to grow up in a safe, private environment.

What advantage does the District 1 location give families with school-age children?

Grand Marina sits in District 1, close to international schools and major hospitals, with Ba Son Metro station only about 250 m away — making school runs and medical needs convenient.

The central location is an important part of the family experience. The off-site amenities that parents care about include:

  • International schools: BIS (District 3), ISSP (Binh Thanh), AIS (Thu Thiem) — all a short drive away.
  • Hospitals: Vinmec Central Park, FMP, UMC (District 5), FV (District 7) for children's medical needs.
  • Family outings nearby: the Saigon Zoo & Botanical Gardens within 500 m — an ideal weekend destination for little ones.
  • Metro Line 1: Ba Son station ~250 m away; about 1 minute to Ben Thanh.

Short distances to schools and hospitals cut down travel time — something many families value even more than apartment size. Having a Metro station right beside the project (now commercially operating) also lets older children travel safely on their own when needed.

Why is a branded residence a good fit for families?

Branded residences like Grand Marina combine five-star hotel service with long-term living, delivering the safety, comfort and consistency that families with young children value.

The "branded residence" concept means residents enjoy the service and operating standards of a hotel brand — here, Marriott and JW Marriott. For families, this shows up as concierge support for booking services, housekeeping, 24/7 security and amenities maintained to hotel standards. If you want to understand the model, see What are branded residences? and browse the Amenities & services page for the full picture. According to market reports from Knight Frank and Savills (2023–2024), branded residences are typically priced 25–35% above comparable non-branded luxury apartments — this is a market reference, not a promise, and actual outcomes depend on the project, timing and policy.
