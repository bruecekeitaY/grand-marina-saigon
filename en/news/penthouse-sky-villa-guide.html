Grand Marina Penthouse & Sky Villa: The Bespoke Top Tier

At the very top of the Marriott towers on the Saigon River sits a different world: bespoke penthouses and sky villas with private plunge pools, private lifts, a cinema and 270° views. This is the rarest tier of all — made for ultra-high-net-worth buyers who want something that cannot be replicated.

How do Grand Marina penthouses and sky villas differ from standard units?

The penthouse and sky villa form the bespoke top tier of the project — the largest floor areas, the highest positions and a personalised design, set entirely apart from the standard 1–3 bedroom units.

At Grand Marina Saigon, the unit range runs from 1-bedroom homes (~50–60 m²) up to 3-bedroom layouts (~110–140 m²) plus Dual-Key. But at the very top of each tower sit two distinct product lines: the Sky Villa / Duplex (~180–250 m²) and the Penthouse (from 300 m²). These are not simply "bigger apartments" — they are a different level of living in terms of space, privacy and customisation.

The key distinction lies in the word "bespoke". While standard units are handed over with a single, consistent Marriott material specification, penthouses let the owner shape the space — from layout to finishing materials. If you want the full picture first, see the Residences & layouts page to compare every unit type.

Just how rare is a Grand Marina penthouse?

The Lake tower (Marriott brand) holds just 2 penthouses out of hundreds of units — about 0.5% of supply — making this the scarcest tier in the entire project.

That scarcity is deliberate, not accidental. Within Lake's product mix the numbers say it all:

  • 1-bedroom: 72 units (16%)
  • 2-bedroom: 60 units (14%)
  • 3-bedroom: 65 units (15%)
  • Dual-Key: 44 units (10%)
  • Penthouse: just 2 units (0.5%)

When a product line makes up only half a percent of all units, it becomes a "collection of one" — with virtually no equivalent substitute anywhere in central District 1. For UHNW buyers, it is this one-of-a-kind status — not floor area alone — that defines the value. Sky villas are more numerous than penthouses but still sit within a strictly limited group of homes on the highest floors.

What does the bespoke top tier come with?

Grand Marina penthouses are personalised with a private plunge pool, a private lift, a garden, a cinema room and 270° views over the river and the city.

Unlike standard units, penthouses are not confined to a fixed set of amenities. Signature privileges typically include:

  • Private plunge pool: on the home's own terrace, never shared
  • Private lift: direct access from the lobby to the residence for maximum privacy and security
  • Sky garden & cinema: self-contained entertainment within the home itself
  • 270° outlook: embracing the Saigon River, the 657-hectare Thu Thiem New Urban Area and the District 1 skyline

All of this still sits on the project's Marriott handover standard: marble/engineered-wood floors, Poggenpohl/Boffi kitchens with Miele/Gaggenau appliances, Toto/Duravit/Hansgrohe bathrooms, Daikin VRV air conditioning and app-based smart-home control. To understand this elite material standard, read Inside Grand Marina's Marriott Handover: Finishes & Appliances.

Which tower holds the penthouses, and what are the views like?

Penthouses and sky villas occupy the top floors of the tallest towers such as Lake (47 floors, 176.4 m) and Cove (47 floors, 184.2 m), where the outlook over the Saigon River and Thu Thiem is at its widest and most open.

Each tower in the project carries a different brand and interior designer — Lake is the Marriott brand (interiors by HBA), while Cove is JW Marriott (interiors by AB Concept of Hong Kong, architecture by ATKINS of the UK). That difference shapes the finishing style at penthouse level. To choose the right tower, read Grand Marina Towers Compared: Lake, Lagoon, Cove & Sea.

At top-tier height, orientation defines the living experience. The river view sweeps across the bend of the Saigon River and the Thu Thiem New Urban Area, while the city view faces the District 1 skyline and Landmark 81. Penthouses typically exploit a 270° angle to capture both. If you're torn between the two, Grand Marina River View vs City View: Choosing Your Outlook will help you decide.

Who is it for, and what about legal ownership?

This tier suits ultra-high-net-worth (UHNW) buyers who want a one-of-a-kind residence to live in or hold as a legacy asset; foreigners can still buy within the 30%-per-building cap under 50-year renewable ownership.

Penthouses and sky villas are not mass-market investment products — they are aimed at buyers seeking a personal signature and absolute privacy. On the legal side, Vietnamese buyers receive a long-term pink book with full rights to transfer, lease and inherit; foreigners hold 50-year ownership (renewable under the law), with transfer and lease permitted and bilingual paperwork support.

On market value, this is Vietnam's first branded-residences project. According to Knight Frank and Savills reports (2023–2024), branded real estate is typically priced 25–35% above comparable non-branded property in the same location — a market reference, not a profit promise, with actual outcomes depending on the project, timing and policy.
