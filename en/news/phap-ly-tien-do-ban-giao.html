One of the first questions buyers ask about Grand Marina Saigon is simple: is it built, is it handed over, and is the legal paperwork clean? Yes — all four towers (Lake, Lagoon, Cove and Sea) have been handed over and residents have moved in.

Is Grand Marina's legal status clean?

The project is developed by Masterise Homes, has met handover and title-issuance conditions, and is among the most transparent legal profiles in District 1's luxury segment.

A project can only be handed over and occupied once it has passed inspection and satisfied the legal conditions required by regulation. The fact that all four Grand Marina towers have been handed over and have residents living in them is a strong signal that the legal file is on the right track. This is a major difference from off-plan projects, where buyers have to trust a future promise.

For an overview of the developer and the Marriott partnership, see the About the project page to understand why this is a clearly established asset.

How do the pink book and ownership rights work?

Vietnamese buyers receive a long-term pink book; foreigners hold 50-year ownership, renewable under the law, with a cap of 30% of units per building.

Ownership at Grand Marina is structured by buyer type as follows:

  • Vietnamese buyers: receive a long-term pink book (sổ hồng) with full rights to use, transfer, lease and inherit; bank loans of up to 70% are available.
  • Foreign buyers: hold 50-year ownership, renewable per Vietnamese law; capped at 30% of units per building; transfer and lease are permitted, with bilingual paperwork support.
  • Officetel: 50-year tenure.

Because the project is already handed over, the title-issuance process is far smoother than for a project under construction. That said, the issuance status for any specific unit can differ depending on its file, so you should check the legal status of the exact unit you are interested in before committing. This is general reference information, not advice for your individual situation.

Why buy a handed-over asset instead of an off-plan one?

Buying a completed asset removes timeline risk, lets you see the real build quality and means you can lease it out immediately.

Compared with buying an under-construction apartment, a handed-over asset like Grand Marina offers several clear advantages:

  • No more risk of late handover or a design that differs from what was promised.
  • You see the real finishing quality: stone/engineered-wood floors, Poggenpohl/Boffi kitchens with Miele/Gaggenau appliances, Daikin VRV air conditioning.
  • Marriott services are already running: 24/7 concierge, multi-layer security, a river-facing infinity pool.
  • You can lease it out right away or move in immediately — no waiting for cash flow.

One thing worth noting is that Grand Marina runs on a branded-residence model, with a 20-year Marriott operating contract and the management fee subsidized by the developer for the first three years.
