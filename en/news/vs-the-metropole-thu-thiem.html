Grand Marina vs The Metropole Thu Thiem: A Real Comparison

Both sit by the Saigon River, yet they follow two different philosophies: one is a Marriott branded residence with 24/7 hotel operations, the other is SonKim Land's design-led apartment collection. This guide helps you compare and choose well.

  1. Home
  2. News
  3. Grand Marina vs The Metropole Thu Thiem

When shopping for a luxury riverside apartment in central Ho Chi Minh City, many buyers put Grand Marina Saigon side by side with The Metropole Thu Thiem. These are genuinely different products: Grand Marina is a Marriott & JW Marriott branded residence developed by Masterise Homes at Ba Son in District 1, while The Metropole is SonKim Land's design-led luxury apartment collection at Thu Thiem in Thu Duc City, without a hotel brand operating it. This article gives you an honest comparison so the core differences are clear.

What is the core difference between the two projects?

Grand Marina is a branded residence run by Marriott as service operator, while The Metropole is a high-end design-led apartment by SonKim Land with no hotel brand operating it.

This is the single most important distinction. Grand Marina is Vietnam's first Marriott & JW Marriott branded residences, backed by a 20-year operating contract with Marriott International. The Metropole Thu Thiem is highly regarded by investors for its architecture and riverside location at Thu Thiem, but its services are typically delivered by a high-standard building management team rather than a global hotel group.

Put simply, buying Grand Marina means buying into the Marriott service ecosystem; buying The Metropole means buying a beautifully designed property in a strong location. To understand this concept first, you can read our guide on what are branded residences? before deciding.

Location: how do Ba Son District 1 and Thu Thiem differ?

Grand Marina sits at Ba Son in District 1 — the existing city core — while The Metropole is across the river at Thu Thiem, a new urban area still taking shape.

Grand Marina is at 02 Ton Duc Thang, Ben Nghe Ward, District 1, about 200 m from the Saigon River and roughly 250 m from the Ba Son metro station (Ben Thanh–Suoi Tien line, now operating). The Opera House, Nguyen Hue walking street and Ben Thanh Market are all within 1 km, and the Botanical Garden within 500 m.

  • Grand Marina: the established District 1 core, with mature infrastructure and amenities, walkable to the city center.
  • The Metropole: the 657-hectare Thu Thiem New Urban Area across the river, open and spacious, looking back toward the District 1 skyline.

Interestingly, Grand Marina looks directly across the river toward the Thu Thiem New Urban Area, while The Metropole looks back at the District 1 skyline where Grand Marina stands. The completed Thu Thiem Bridge 2 makes the two banks easy to connect. If you prioritise living in the established core with ready infrastructure, Ba Son has the edge; if you prefer a fresh setting with a skyline view, Thu Thiem is appealing.

Operating services: where Grand Marina stands out

Grand Marina has Marriott running 24/7 services with concierge, room service and Bonvoy, which is the biggest difference from The Metropole.

At Grand Marina, residents enjoy services operated directly by Marriott:

  • 24/7 concierge and in-residence dining (room service) from the JW Marriott kitchen.
  • Housekeeping, laundry, valet parking and multi-layer 24/7 security (CCTV, card + biometric access).
  • Marriott Bonvoy benefits: privileges at 8,000+ Marriott hotels, point earning and room upgrades.

This is a five-star hotel service package built into your home — something an unbranded high-end project like The Metropole typically does not offer at the same level. The Metropole still has professional management and good in-house amenities, but the "living in a hotel" experience is the signature of branded residences.

Handover and interiors: how are they finished?

Grand Marina is handed over fully fitted to Marriott standard with Poggenpohl/Boffi kitchens and Miele/Gaggenau appliances, a finish that is hard for a standard project to match.

Grand Marina units come with full fitted interiors: marble/engineered-wood floors, Poggenpohl/Boffi kitchens with Miele/Gaggenau appliances, Toto/Duravit/Hansgrohe bathrooms, Daikin VRV air conditioning, imported double-glazed doors and app-based smart home. Interiors are designed by renowned firms such as HBA and AB Concept (Hong Kong).

The Metropole Thu Thiem is also well regarded for its build quality and SonKim Land's refined design language, but each phase and project has a different handover package. You should cross-check the specific material list of each unit before comparing. This is also why many buyers also look at Grand Marina vs The River Thu Thiem (Masterise) — another project by the same developer, Masterise, in Thu Thiem.

Price and investment potential: how to read it

According to reference data from Knight Frank and Savills (2023–2024), branded residences are typically priced 25–35% above comparable non-branded luxury apartments, reflecting brand value and operated services.

At Grand Marina, indicative prices range around: 1BR roughly 400–500 million VND/m² (from ~20 billion VND), 2BR from ~35 billion, 3BR from ~60 billion. Payment options include ~25/75 or 30/70, bank loan (grace period / 0% interest to handover), or fast payment of 95–100% with an 8–12% discount. These are reference figures that change by sales phase.

  • Grand Marina: higher price, but with the Marriott brand, operated services and an established District 1 address.
  • The Metropole: usually a somewhat softer price point, suited to buyers who prioritise design and the Thu Thiem setting.

It is worth stressing: the price gap above is only a market reference, not a promise of returns. Actual outcomes depend on the specific project, timing and policy. If you want a broader view on branded-residence pricing, see Grand Marina vs Bangkok Branded Residences or the overview on the about the project page.

Which one should you choose?

Choose Grand Marina if you value the Marriott brand, 24/7 operated services and a District 1 core location; choose The Metropole if you love the Thu Thiem setting and SonKim Land's design.

There is no absolute "right" answer — the decision depends on your priorities: owner-occupier or rental investment, budget, and how much you value operated services. The general advice is to review the legal documents, price list and material list of each specific unit before placing a deposit, rather than comparing on headline specs alone.
