*Source of truth extracted from the existing VI pages (about, location, residences, amenities, pricing, faq, news, news/branded-residences). All figures are "as stated on site" unless noted.*

## 1. Project identity
- **Name:** Grand Marina Saigon
- **Developer (chủ đầu tư):** Masterise Homes (part of Masterise Group)
- **Brand partner / operator:** Marriott International — brands **Marriott** and **JW Marriott**
- **Positioning:** Vietnam's **first Marriott & JW Marriott Branded Residences** ("căn hộ hàng hiệu đầu tiên tại Việt Nam")
- **Location:** Ba Son, riverside (sông Sài Gòn), District 1 (Quận 1), HCMC. Official address: **Số 02 Tôn Đức Thắng, Phường Bến Nghé, Quận 1, TP.HCM**
- **Geo (schema):** lat 10.7891, lng 106.7058
- **Marriott operating contract:** **20 years** (developer subsidizes management fee for first 3 years; after that body of residents may renew or change operator)

## 2. Towers & phases (4 towers)
- **Lake** — Marriott brand, **Phase 2**, **47 floors / 176.4 m**. Land area 15,723.8 m². Sub-developer "Hưng Phát Investment"; interior design by **HBA**; contractor **Newtecons**. Handed over **03/2023**. ("Prime Collection")
- **Lagoon** — JW Marriott, **Phase 1**, includes apartments + Officetel. Handover **12/2023**. ("Legacy Collection")
- **Cove** — JW Marriott, **Phase 1**, **47 floors / 184.2 m**. Sub-developer "Elegance"; interiors by **AB Concept (Hong Kong)**; architecture by **ATKINS (UK)**. Handover **12/2023**. ("Legacy Collection")
- **Sea** — Phase 1; JW Marriott apartments + **Marriott Officetel**. Handover **12/2023**. ("Legacy Collection")
- **Phase 1 land area:** 16,866.5 m² (Lagoon, Cove, Sea). **All phases handed over;** residents living in, Marriott concierge operating; active secondary/resale market (2024–2026).
- Milestones: Masterise×Marriott partnership announced **2020–2021**; Sales Gallery opened **2021**; Lake construction completed **2022**; Metro Line 1 now commercially operating.
