## 1. Project identity
- **Name:** Grand Marina Saigon
- **Developer (chủ đầu tư):** Masterise Homes (part of Masterise Group)
- **Brand partner / operator:** Marriott International — brands **Marriott** and **JW Marriott**
- **Positioning:** Vietnam's **first Marriott & JW Marriott Branded Residences** ("căn hộ hàng hiệu đầu tiên tại Việt Nam")
- **Location:** Ba Son, riverside (sông Sài Gòn), District 1 (Quận 1), HCMC. Official address: **Số 02 Tôn Đức Thắng, Phường Bến Nghé, Quận 1, TP.HCM**
- **Geo (schema):** lat 10.7891, lng 106.7058
- **Marriott operating contract:** **20 years** (developer subsidizes management fee for first 3 years; after that body of residents may renew or change operator)

## 2. Towers & phases (4 towers)
- **Lake** — Marriott brand, **Phase 2**, **47 floors / 176.4 m**. Land area 15,723.8 m². Sub-developer "Hưng Phát Investment"; interior design by **HBA**; contractor **Newtecons**. Grand opening 11/2023 (an opening, not a unit handover date). ("Prime Collection")
- **Lagoon** — JW Marriott, **Phase 1**, includes apartments + Officetel. No opening or sale announced (see accuracy review). ("Legacy Collection")
- **Cove** — JW Marriott, **Phase 1**, **47 floors / 184.2 m**. Sub-developer "Elegance"; interiors by **AB Concept (Hong Kong)**; architecture by **ATKINS (UK)**. No opening or sale announced (see accuracy review). ("Legacy Collection")
- **Sea** — Phase 1; JW Marriott apartments + **Marriott Officetel**. Officially opened Q1 2025 (VnExpress, 2025-02-26; an opening, not a unit handover date). ("Legacy Collection")
- **Phase 1 land area:** 16,866.5 m² (Lagoon, Cove, Sea). Lake and Sea are sold directly by the developer and also on resale (2026-09-18); do not claim that all towers are handed over or that resale dominates.
- Milestones: Masterise×Marriott partnership announced **2020–2021**; Sales Gallery opened **2021**; Lake construction completed **2022**; Metro Line 1 now commercially operating.

## 4. Amenities & services
Marriott-run services: **24/7 concierge, in-residence dining/room service (JW Marriott kitchen), housekeeping & laundry, valet parking, 24/7 multi-layer security** (CCTV, card + biometric access). **Marriott Bonvoy** benefits (discounts at 8,000+ Marriott hotels, point earning, Vacation Club access, room upgrades). Facilities: **Sky Infinity Pool** (river-facing) + kids/family pool, jacuzzi, poolside bar; **Technogym** fitness + yoga/pilates/functional/boxing; spa & wellness (massage, sauna, steam, herbal bath); **Sky Lounge & Library** (2,000+ books), Boardroom & Private Dining, Cigar Lounge & Whisky Bar, **Cinema Room** (4K, Dolby Atmos); Kids Club, outdoor playground, Mommy & Baby lounge; co-working, meeting rooms, business center; Riverside Garden, Central Lake, Sky Garden, **Yacht Club access**; podium shopping arcade (boutiques, JW Marriott signature restaurant, café/bakery, 24/7 mini-mart, salon).

## 5. Location & connectivity
- **Saigon River 200 m; Metro Line 1 Ba Son station ~250 m** (Bến Thành–Suối Tiên line, now operating).
- Distances (as stated): Thảo Cầm Viên within 500 m; city landmarks (Opera House, Nguyễn Huệ, Bến Thành) within 1 km; Thủ Thiêm Tunnel ~2 km; Landmark 81 ~4 km; Tân Sơn Nhất Airport ~8 km. Shopping: Vincom Đồng Khởi 1 km, Saigon Centre/Takashimaya 1.2 km, Diamond Plaza 1.3 km, Bến Thành Market 1.5 km.
- Metro times (as stated): Bến Thành 1 min, Opera House 30 sec, An Phú/Thảo Điền 5–8 min, Suối Tiên 25 min.
- Schools: BIS (Q3), ISSP (Bình Thạnh), AIS (Thủ Thiêm). Hospitals: FV (Q7), UMC (Q5), Vinmec Central Park, FMP. Faces **Thủ Thiêm New Urban Area** (657 ha) across the river; Thủ Thiêm Bridge 2 completed.

## 6. Foreign ownership (as stated on site)
- Vietnamese buyers: **long-term pink book (sổ hồng lâu dài)**, full rights to use/transfer/lease/inherit; bank loan up to 70%.
- Foreigners: **50-year ownership, renewable per Vietnamese law**; **cap of 30% of units per building**; transfer & lease allowed; bilingual paperwork/translation support. Officetel: 50-year tenure.
